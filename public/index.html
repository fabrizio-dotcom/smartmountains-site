The problem

Decisions are made on incomplete data

Municipalities, civil protection, park authorities and infrastructure operators make choices about roads, settlements and water with partial information. A satellite pass here, a manual survey there, a forecast that stops at the valley floor. Each source tells part of the story, and none of them was designed to work with the others.

“The data exists. The system that turns it into a local decision mostly does not.”

How it works

Monitor, predict, act

01

Monitor

Satellite radar (InSAR, Sentinel-1) shows ground movement across a whole slope. Corner reflectors and ground sensors confirm it where it matters and cover what the radar cannot see.

02

Predict

Models are calibrated to each territory, its history and its measurements on the ground, so a signal can be read as a trend instead of an alarm.

03

Act

Civil protection, municipalities and infrastructure managers receive decision support with the evidence behind it, and can see how sure we are.

The model

Infrastructure, owned with the territory

Smart Mountains is built as infrastructure, not sold as software. The value sits in a monitoring network that stays in place, run by people who live there.

Built locally

Each site is set up with local partners, through a local joint venture or business network, so the cost of switching it on becomes local work and training.

Long horizons

Service agreements run for years, not weeks. What we offer is a network and the continuity that keeps it reliable.

Data stays local

We do not sell data. Local authorities keep control of what is measured on their land.

A profession for the valley

Running a network takes hydraulics, electronics and geomatics. We want to grow that skill set locally, with schools, universities and training bodies, as a new Alpine profession: the territory steward. Read more

We say what we cannot see.

Radar has blind spots. Steep, wooded or snow-covered slopes can lose signal, and some areas have no historical archive. Every site brief states what is covered, what is not, and what we will check on the ground.
